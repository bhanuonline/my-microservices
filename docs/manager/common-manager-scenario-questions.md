# Common Manager Interview Scenario Questions

Top scenarios to prepare — grouped by theme. Each entry lists the question and the key signals interviewers watch for.

---

## Category 1 — People Management

### Q1. Underperformer in the team
> "You have a team member who is consistently missing deadlines and producing low-quality work. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Empathy + data + clear expectations + PIP if needed.

---

### Q2. Conflict between two team members
> "Two of your engineers are constantly clashing — one thinks the other's code is bad, the other feels micromanaged. How do you resolve it?"

**Watch for:** 1:1s first, then joint discussion, focus on behavior not personality.

---

### Q3. Star performer wants to leave
> "Your best engineer tells you he's got an offer from another company and is planning to leave. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Understand *why* (money? growth? manager?), don't just counter-offer, retain via growth plan.

---

### Q4. Team member asks for promotion but isn't ready
> "A team member walks into your 1:1 and demands a promotion, but you don't think they're ready. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Honest feedback + clear growth roadmap + timeline, not vague "soon."

---

### Q5. New hire is not fitting in
> "You hired an engineer 2 months ago. Skills are okay, but the team says he doesn't collaborate well. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Early feedback, buddy system, don't wait till it's too late.

---

## Category 2 — Delivery & Release

### Q6. Mid-sprint scope change from stakeholders
> "Two weeks into a 4-week sprint, the product owner adds a critical new feature. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Push back with data — show trade-offs (drop X, delay Y, or add capacity).

---

### Q7. Production incident during release
> "You just deployed to production and P1 incident is reported. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Rollback first, blame-free RCA later, communicate to stakeholders.

---

### Q8. Your team missed the deadline
> "Your team missed a major release deadline. Your boss is upset. What do you say?"

**Watch for:** Own it, don't blame team, present the RCA + prevention plan.

---

### Q9. Estimate vs. Reality mismatch
> "Your team estimated 5 days but it's taken 10 and still not done. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Understand blocker, re-plan, communicate early, learn from estimation gap.

---

## Category 3 — Stakeholder Management

### Q10. Business wants it faster, team says it's not possible
> "Business is pushing for delivery in 2 weeks. Your team says 6 weeks minimum. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Negotiate — MVP first, phased release, cut scope. Don't cave, don't refuse.

---

### Q11. Disagreement with your own manager
> "Your manager wants to prioritize Feature A. You believe Feature B is more critical. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Disagree with data, in private. Once decision is made, commit fully.

---

### Q12. Product Owner keeps changing priorities
> "Every sprint, the PO changes priorities mid-sprint. Team is frustrated. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Protect the team, set boundaries via Scrum ceremonies, escalate if needed.

---

## Category 4 — Technical Leadership

### Q13. Team disagrees on architecture
> "Two senior engineers strongly disagree on the design approach. How do you break the tie?"

**Watch for:** Let them debate with data, ask questions, decide based on trade-offs, document why.

---

### Q14. Legacy system vs. rewrite
> "The codebase is legacy and painful. Team wants a full rewrite. Business wants new features. How do you balance?"

**Watch for:** Strangler pattern, incremental refactor, no big-bang rewrite.

---

### Q15. Tech debt is piling up
> "Team keeps saying tech debt is a problem, but business keeps pushing features. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Allocate **20% capacity** to tech debt every sprint. Make it visible in metrics.

---

## Category 5 — Team Growth & Culture

### Q16. Team is demotivated
> "You notice your team's morale is low — attrition risk. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** 1:1s to understand root cause (workload, growth, recognition), act visibly.

---

### Q17. Introducing a new process (e.g., code reviews, CI/CD)
> "You want to introduce mandatory code reviews, but the team pushes back saying it slows them down. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Explain "why," pilot small, measure impact, adjust based on feedback.

---

### Q18. Building a team from scratch
> "You've been asked to build a new team of 5 engineers for a greenfield project. Walk me through your approach."

**Watch for:** Hire for diversity of skills, senior first, culture-fit, ramp-up plan.

---

## Category 6 — Crisis / Difficult Situations

### Q19. You have to lay off a team member
> "HR tells you that due to budget cuts, one engineer must be let go. How do you decide and communicate?"

**Watch for:** Fair criteria (performance, role redundancy), respectful conversation, support with references.

---

### Q20. Security breach / data leak
> "A critical vulnerability was found in your service, and there's a suspected data leak. What's your first hour?"

**Watch for:** Contain first, communicate to security/leadership, RCA, prevention.

---

### Q21. Your team member made a costly mistake
> "A junior engineer accidentally deleted a production database table. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Restore first, no blame in public, learning + prevention (permissions, backups).

---

### Q22. You disagree with a company decision
> "Leadership decides to sunset a product your team built. Team is upset. How do you handle it?"

**Watch for:** Acknowledge feelings, explain business context, redirect energy to next mission.

---

## Category 7 — Hiring & Interviews

### Q23. Candidate is strong technically but weak culturally
> "Your candidate aced the tech round but the culture-fit interviewer says he's arrogant. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Don't compromise on culture — one bad hire poisons the team.

---

### Q24. Team is overloaded, hiring is slow
> "Team is burning out, but hiring pipeline is dry. What do you do?"

**Watch for:** Cut scope, negotiate deadlines, use contractors as short-term relief, escalate.

## Universal STAR Framework for All Scenarios

```
   S  — Situation:   Set context in 1 sentence
   T  — Task:        What was your responsibility
   A  — Action:      3–5 concrete steps YOU took
   R  — Result:      Measurable outcome + what you learned
```
