RULES━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━1. Water:    - Aim for ~2–3 litres/day.    - Increase somewhat on very hot/sweaty exercise days.    - Don't force excessive water.2. Protein:    - Include protein in every main meal.    - Eggs / dal / paneer / chicken / fish / curd are good options.3. Rice:    - You DON'T need to completely stop rice.    - Keep it around 1 cup cooked when having rice.4. Roti:    - Prefer 2 medium roti rather than unlimited portions.5. Vegetables:    - Aim for 1–2 cups at lunch and dinner.6. Avoid/limit:    - Soft drinks    - Sweets    - Chips    - Deep-fried food    - Excess biscuits    - Sugary tea/coffee    - Frequent fast food7. One flexible meal:    - You can have one normal/favorite meal on Sunday.    - Keep the portion reasonable and don't turn it into an entire cheat day.8. Fat loss:    - Target roughly 0.25–0.75 kg/week rather than trying to lose weight very quickly.    - Thigh fat will reduce as overall body fat reduces.
