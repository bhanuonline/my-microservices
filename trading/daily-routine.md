- Use **Sensibull / Opstra / TradingView paper account** to simulate trades

## PHASE 3 — LIVE WATCHING (9:30 AM – 3:00 PM)

### The 3 Questions (ask on every 5-min candle close)

**Q1 — Is price approaching one of my 9 lines?**
- Within 20 points? Pay attention.
- Farther? Ignore, keep waiting.

**Q2 — Did price wick beyond a line, then close back inside?**
- Wick above line + close below = **BSL sweep**
- Wick below line + close above = **SSL sweep**
- Wick AND close both beyond = **breakout** (different setup)

**Q3 — After the sweep, did the next candle break the recent swing?**

> **"Recent swing" = the most recent noticeable pivot high or low in the last 5–10 candles.** A "pivot" is a candle where price clearly turned — 2 lower highs on each side (for a swing high) or 2 higher lows on each side (for a swing low).

- Sweep of high + next candle breaks recent pivot low = **CHOCH down** ✓
- Sweep of low + next candle breaks recent pivot high = **CHOCH up** ✓

**All 3 YES → setup found → message me with template below.**

## PHASE 4 — OPTIONS-SPECIFIC RULES (READ CAREFULLY)

You are trading weekly options. Spot analysis tells you *direction*; options rules tell you *how* to trade it.

### Strike Selection

| Setup type | Strike to buy | Why |
|-----------|---------------|-----|
| Strong directional (Setup A, B) | **ATM** (at-the-money) | Highest delta, moves 1:1 with spot |
| Momentum breakout | **ATM + 1 strike OTM** | Cheaper premium, decent movement |
| Scalp (5-10 pt move) | **Deep OTM** | Cheap, high % gain, high risk |
| Overnight hold | **Never** — see below | |

### The Golden Options Rules

1. **NEVER hold weekly options overnight without a hedge.** Theta eats you.
2. **NEVER buy options after 2:30 PM on Thursday** (expiry day) — premium collapses.
3. **On Thursday morning (expiry day):** OTM options can go from ₹50 to ₹0 in minutes. **Only buy ATM/ITM if you must trade.**
4. **SL in premium terms, not spot terms.** If you buy CE at ₹100, SL at ₹70 (30% loss). Don't wait for spot to hit some level.
5. **Target in premium terms.** Book 50% at 1:1, trail rest.
6. **IV crush after events** (RBI, results): premium drops even if direction is right. **Avoid buying before events.**
7. **Position size:** never risk more than **1% of capital** on a single option trade.

### Position Sizing Formula (Options)

```
Lots = (Capital × 1%) / (Premium SL × 75)

Example:
Capital: ₹5,00,000
Max risk: ₹5,000
Buy CE at ₹100, SL at ₹70 → risk = ₹30 per share
Lots = 5000 / (30 × 75) = 2.2 → round DOWN to 2 lots
```

### Thursday (Expiry Day) Behavior — MEMORIZE

- **9:15–11:00 AM:** Normal setups work, but book profits fast
- **11:00 AM–1:00 PM:** Choppy, theta accelerating, avoid new entries
- **1:00–2:30 PM:** Only trade ATM options, tight SL, book at 1:1
- **After 2:30 PM:** **CLOSE ALL POSITIONS. Do not enter new ones.**
- **After 3:00 PM:** Premium can go to zero. Watch, don't touch.

## EMERGENCY SCENARIOS — WHAT TO DO

| Situation | What to do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Power/internet dies **before entry** | Skip the trade. Don't force it. |
| Power/internet dies **during trade** | Call broker → square off phone order → don't hedge without seeing chart |
| You missed 9:15–9:30 OR marking | Use 9:30–10:00 range as substitute OR |
| You entered and immediately regret | **Exit at market price NOW.** Don't rationalize. |
| SL was ₹70, price is now ₹65 | Exit. Don't move SL to ₹50. Rules are rules. |
| You made 3 winning trades in a row | **STOP.** Overconfidence is the next loss. |
| Broker shows wrong price / stuck order | Call broker helpline immediately. Screenshot everything. |

## RULES WHILE WATCHING (memorize)

1. **First 15 min (9:15–9:30):** No trades. Only observe.
2. **Never chase.** Missed entry = missed trade. Next one comes.
3. **Only 3 trades per day max.** After 3, close the chart.
4. **Stop after 2 losing trades.** Come back tomorrow.
5. **Lunch (12:00–1:00 PM):** Usually choppy. Walk away.
6. **Last 30 min (3:00–3:30):** Only exit, never enter.
7. **No trade without CHOCH confirmation.**
8. **No trade against Bank NIFTY direction.**
9. **No trade with India VIX > 25.**
10. **No trade if you can't state the setup in one sentence.**
