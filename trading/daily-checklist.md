## LIVE WATCHING (9:30 AM – 3:00 PM)

### STEP 9 — Mark swing highs & lows as they form (ongoing)

**This is not a one-time step — do it all day, every 15-30 min.**

Swings are **dynamic** — they form live as price moves. Every time a new swing appears, mark it. When it gets broken, delete it.

**What counts as a swing?**

```
Swing HIGH pattern:                Swing LOW pattern:
                                        
     ● SH  ← peak candle             ── ── ── 
    /  \                              \      /
   ↑    ↓                              ↓    ↑
  2      2                             2    2
  lower  lower                         higher higher
  candles                              candles
  each side                            each side
                                        ● SL  ← trough candle
```

**Rule:** A candle is a swing high if it has at least **2 candles with lower highs on the left AND 2 candles with lower highs on the right**. Same reverse logic for swing low.

**How to mark:**
- Use a **short horizontal line** (not full-width like PDH) — just 5-10 candles wide
- Label **SH** (swing high) or **SL** (swing low)
- Use a **different color** (e.g. yellow) so it doesn't mix with your fixed levels

**When to delete:**
- Once price breaks through the SH or SL, delete the line (it's no longer liquidity — it's been taken)
- Keep only the **last 2-3 unbroken** SH and SL on chart (delete older ones to avoid clutter)

**Why bother:**
- Each SH above current price = mini **buy-side liquidity pool** (short stops)
- Each SL below current price = mini **sell-side liquidity pool** (long stops)
- Price often sweeps these small pools before moving to the big PDH/PDL

**Chart lines: 9 fixed + 2-4 dynamic swings = ~11-13 lines total**

### STEP 10 — Wait for alert
Don't stare at chart. Wait for **alert to fire** on any of your 9 fixed lines, OR spot price approaching an unmarked SH/SL.

When something triggers → go to Step 11.

---

### STEP 11 — Check: did price WICK past the line?
When price touches a line (fixed OR swing), watch that 5-min candle **close**:

- ✅ Wick went past line + candle closed **back inside** = **SWEEP** (good, go to Step 12)
- ❌ Wick and body both closed past line = **breakout** (skip, wait for next alert)
- ❌ Price bounced without wicking = **rejection only** (skip)

---

### STEP 12 — Check: did the NEXT candle break recent swing?
After the sweep candle, watch the NEXT 5-min candle.

You already have swings marked from Step 9 — check if the next candle breaks one:

- Sweep of HIGH + next candle broke a marked **SL** = **CHOCH DOWN** → short setup
- Sweep of LOW + next candle broke a marked **SH** = **CHOCH UP** → long setup

If neither happened → skip, wait for next alert.

## AFTER MY REPLY

### STEP 15 — If I say YES: pick your option strike
- Strong setup → buy **ATM** (at-the-money strike)
- Momentum breakout → buy **ATM + 1 OTM**
- Note the premium price (e.g. ₹100)

---

### STEP 16 — Set your SL and target BEFORE entry
- SL = premium down 30% (e.g. bought at ₹100 → SL at ₹70)
- Target 1 = premium up 50% (₹150) — book 50% here
- Target 2 = premium up 100% (₹200) — trail rest

**Never enter without knowing exit prices in advance.**

---

### STEP 17 — Enter the trade
- Buy the option
- **Immediately place SL order** (stop-loss market order at ₹70)
- Set alert at Target 1 (₹150)

---

### STEP 18 — Manage the trade
Only 2 actions allowed:
- Alert fires at Target 1 → sell 50%, move SL to entry (₹100 = free trade)
- Alert fires at Target 2 → sell rest

**Not allowed:**
- Moving SL down (increasing loss)
- Adding to loser
- Watching every tick — you'll panic exit
