# NIFTY Daily Notes

> Fill this every trading morning at **8:30 AM**.
> Copy the **BLANK TEMPLATE** block below, paste it at the top of the **DAILY ENTRIES** section, replace date and blanks.
> Newest day stays on top so you don't scroll.

---

## HOW TO USE

1. Open this file at 8:30 AM
2. Select the entire **BLANK TEMPLATE** block below (between `<<<` and `>>>`)
3. Copy it
4. Paste it right below the "DAILY ENTRIES" heading (at the top, above yesterday's entry)
5. Fill in today's date and all the blanks as you go through `daily-checklist.md` steps 1-8
6. At end of day, this becomes your searchable history
